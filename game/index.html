DUSKSTRIKE
Pier 7 · Dusk Harbor · 3v3 Tactical FPS

Defend the cargo terminal. The RAIDERS are coming for Pier 7 at dusk — stop the bomb, or lose the harbor.

Best played with mouse + keyboard · Chrome / WebGL 2

How to Play

Objective

You are GUARD. The RAIDERS carry a bomb: stop them planting it at Site A (Warehouse) or Site B (Pier) — or defuse it before the 30-second fuse runs out. Win a round by eliminating the enemy team, defusing the bomb, or holding the sites until the clock runs out. First to 5 rounds wins the match.

Controls

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look
  • Click Fire
  • R Reload
  • 123 Switch weapon
  • Q / Wheel Next weapon
  • G Throw grenade
  • Ctrl / C Crouch
  • Shift Sprint
  • B Buy menu (buy phase only)
  • E (hold) Plant / defuse the bomb
  • Esc Pause

Tips

  • Stop moving before you shoot — accuracy beats speed.
  • Headshots deal double damage.
  • Win a round: +$1600. A kill: +$200. Spend it in the buy menu.
  • Grenades are loud — every bot nearby hears the blast.
